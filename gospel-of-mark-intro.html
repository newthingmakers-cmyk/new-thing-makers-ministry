01 — Overview

What is the Gospel of Mark?

The Gospel of Mark presents Jesus Christ through His ministry, teachings, suffering, death, and resurrection.

Key Scripture: Mark 10:45

The Son of Man came not to be served but to serve.

02 — Author and Background

Who wrote the Gospel of Mark?

Early Christian tradition associates this Gospel with John Mark, although the Gospel itself does not name its author directly.

Mark is mentioned in several New Testament passages, including Acts 12:12 and 2 Timothy 4:11.

03 — Purpose and Main Themes

What should we look for?

  • The identity and authority of Jesus Christ.
  • Jesus' teaching, compassion, and mighty works.
  • The meaning and cost of following Jesus.
  • The suffering, death, and resurrection of Jesus.

04 — How to Study

A simple study approach

  1. Read the passage carefully.
  2. Observe what the passage says.
  3. Understand its meaning in context.
  4. Consider what it teaches about Jesus Christ.
  5. Apply the teaching faithfully in daily life.
