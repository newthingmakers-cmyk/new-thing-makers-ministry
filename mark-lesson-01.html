NEW THING MAKERS MINISTRY

Lesson 1: Introduction to the Gospel of Mark

Discover who Jesus is and what it means to follow Him.

01 — KEY SCRIPTURE

Mark 1:1

“The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.” (Mark 1:1, KJV)

02 — LESSON GOAL

What will we learn?

By the end of this lesson, we should be able to explain why Mark begins by pointing us to Jesus Christ, the Son of God, and describe one practical response to the good news.

Main idea: The Gospel of Mark directs our attention to Jesus—who He is and how we are called to respond to Him.

03 — BIBLICAL TEACHING

Understanding Mark 1:1

A. “The beginning”

Mark begins his Gospel with the significant words, “The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.” These opening words introduce the central focus of his account and invite readers to explore the words and actions of Jesus. As we read, we should pay attention not only to what Jesus teaches, but also to what His actions reveal about who He is.

B. “The gospel”

“Gospel” means good news. Mark’s account announces what God is doing through Jesus Christ. Christian faith is not merely a list of rules; it begins with the good news about Jesus and calls us to trust and follow Him.

C. “Jesus Christ, the Son of God”

Mark names Jesus at the very beginning. “Christ” means the Anointed One, the promised Messiah. “Son of God” identifies Jesus in a unique relationship with the Father and points to His true identity. The Gospel asks us to learn who Jesus is, not simply to admire His teachings.

04 — CHRIST FOCUS

Begin with Jesus

The central person of this Gospel is Jesus Christ. Before asking what we can do for God, we first look at who Jesus is and what God has done through Him. As disciples, we learn from Jesus, trust Him, and follow His way.

05 — REFLECTION AND APPLICATION

Respond to the good news

  1. Read Mark 1:1 slowly two or three times.
  2. Write down what this verse tells you about Jesus.
  3. Choose one way to respond this week: trust Jesus in a difficult situation, obey His Word, or tell someone the good news.
Personal response: Because Jesus is the Son of God, this week I will…

06 — DISCUSSION QUESTIONS

Discuss together

  1. What does the word “gospel” mean?
  2. What does Mark 1:1 tell us about Jesus?
  3. Why is it important to know who Jesus is, not only what He taught?
  4. What is one practical way you can respond to the good news this week?

07 — PRAYER

A prayer to begin

Father, thank You for the good news of Jesus Christ. As I study the Gospel of Mark, help me to see more clearly who Jesus is, to trust Him, and to follow Him faithfully. Let Your Word shape my thoughts, choices, and actions. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
