Lesson 1

Introduction to the Gospel of Mark

Key Scripture: Mark 1:1

The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.

This lesson introduces the Gospel of Mark and its message about Jesus Christ.

1. Lesson Goal

To understand the purpose and main themes of the Gospel of Mark.

2. Biblical Teaching

We will study the context, meaning, Christ-centered message, and practical response of each passage.

The detailed teaching for this lesson will be added as it is prepared.

3. Teaching Video

The teaching video will be available here when it is ready.

4. Reflection and Application

  1. What do I learn about Jesus Christ from this passage?
  2. What truth should shape my life?
  3. What practical step should I take in response?

5. Discussion Questions

  1. Why is it important to study the Gospel of Mark carefully?
  2. How can studying the life of Jesus help us become faithful disciples?

6. Prayer and Response

Pray for understanding, deeper knowledge of Christ, and faithful obedience.
