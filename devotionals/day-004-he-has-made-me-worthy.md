reflection_en: |
  Sometimes, I find myself wondering whether I am still worthy to inherit God's promises because of the mistakes I made in the past. Those past failures have caused me to keep condemning myself, even to this day, and have made me hesitant to take the next steps forward. Feelings of shame often find their way into my heart.
  
  However, as I reflected on Joshua 5:9, where God declared that He had rolled away the reproach of Egypt from the Israelites, I began to understand that my past—whether it was filled with good things or failures—does not have the final say over my future as I walk with God.
  My past may tell my story, but it cannot determine my future.

  Just as God rolled away the reproach of Egypt from the Israelites at Gilgal, He reminds me today that through Christ, my sin has been dealt with and I no longer need to remain under the burden of guilt and shame.

  This truth gives me the strength to rest fully in God's grace. It also encourages me to abandon my old ways of life and continue walking forward with Him into a new life.
