reflection_en: |
  Obeying God is not always easy. Sometimes His Word challenges our personal desires, our plans, or the way we think things should be done. We may understand what God asks of us, yet hesitate because obedience requires sacrifice, humility, or courage.

  Jesus reminds us that obedience is an expression of our love for Him. We obey because we love the Lord and trust His wisdom.

  There may be times when forgiving someone feels difficult, telling the truth comes at a cost, or serving others requires us to put aside our own comfort. In those moments, our love for God is demonstrated through our choices.

  Today, let us not only listen to God's Word but also put it into practice. Even when obedience is difficult, we can ask the Holy Spirit to strengthen us.
