reflection_en: |
  Have you ever been misunderstood by others? In my own life, I have experienced being misunderstood and looked down upon by other people. At times, even teachers whom I respected misunderstood me, and I felt deeply hurt. In those moments, I responded by abandoning the good things I had been doing and deliberately doing things that were wrong to express my dissatisfaction. I did not realize that such responses were not only wrong but were also pushing my life in a direction contrary to God's will.

  In 1 Samuel 1:15, we see Hannah, who did not blame Eli for misunderstanding her. Instead, she humbly explained her true situation and continued to place her hope in God alone. Her response became a blessing to her. Reflecting on Hannah's response has given me new strength. Rather than allowing the words of those who misunderstand and criticize me, or the circumstances I face, to overwhelm me emotionally, I have learned to continue relying on God and to live according to His Word.

  One important lesson I have learned from this is that responding in anger and acting according to our own desires only causes us greater pain. Instead, trusting God and continuing to stand on the right path give us the opportunity to experience His gracious care.
