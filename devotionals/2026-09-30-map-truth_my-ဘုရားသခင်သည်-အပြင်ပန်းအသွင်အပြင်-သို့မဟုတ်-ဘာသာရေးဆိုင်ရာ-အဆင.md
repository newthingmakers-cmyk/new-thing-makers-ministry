reflection_en: >-
  We live in an age where outward appearance and packaging are given great
  importance. Beautiful presentation can easily attract people's attention.
  Since people cannot see what is truly inside, they can often be deceived by
  judging and choosing things based only on their outward appearance. The same
  can happen in our spiritual lives. We may appear good on the outside while
  neglecting the genuine obedience to God's Word that should come from within
  our hearts.


  There were times in my own life when I appeared to be obeying and putting God's Word into practice, but in reality, I was only pretending in order to gain people's approval. Even though others could not see or know, I myself knew that I was doing something wrong. As I reflected on 1 Corinthians 7:19, I began to understand that outward circumstances are not what matter most; what truly matters is keeping and obeying God's Word.


  God does not merely look at our outward actions; He also places great importance on our obedience to His Word. Therefore, I have learned that rather than seeking to look good in the eyes of others, I need to choose what is right before God and live a life marked by genuine obedience.


  Genuine obedience to God with all our hearts is more important than merely displaying a good outward appearance.
