reflection_en: |
  In today's world, people often place great importance on outward appearance and presentation. Something beautifully presented can easily attract people's attention. However, outward appearance does not always reveal what is truly inside. When we judge things only by what we see on the surface, we can easily be misled and fail to recognize the truth.


  The same can happen in our spiritual lives. We may appear to be doing well in our religious practices, yet inwardly, we may neglect genuine obedience to God's Word. Our outward actions may look good, but our hearts may not truly be surrendered to Him.When I reflect on my own life, I realize that there have been times when I appeared to be obeying God's Word, but in reality, I was only trying to maintain a good image before others. Although other people might not have noticed, deep inside, I knew when my actions were not sincere.


  As I meditate on 1 Corinthians 7:19, I am reminded of the Apostle Paul's teaching that outward religious identity, such as circumcision or uncircumcision, is not what truly matters. What matters is keeping God's commandments. This passage reminds us that obedience to God's will is far more important than outward religious signs or practices.God does not merely look at our outward actions; He also cares about our obedience to His Word. Therefore, rather than seeking to appear good in the eyes of others, we should choose to live rightly before God. Our priority should be to have a life that is pleasing to Him.
