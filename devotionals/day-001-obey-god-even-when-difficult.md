reflection_en: |
  Sometimes, even when we are working in an area where we are skilled and experienced, things do not always go as expected. In ministry, no matter how hard I work or how much I sacrifice, I have felt discouraged when things did not turn out as I had hoped. When some friends offered advice, I sometimes dismissed it, thinking, "I already know that. I know how to do it."

  In Luke 5:5, when Jesus told Peter, who was disappointed because he had caught no fish, to let down his net in deep water, Peter responded, "Nevertheless, at Your word I will let down the net." Although this went against Peter's experience, he chose to trust and obey the Lord's word. Through his obedience, Peter witnessed God's power and came to understand Jesus' calling on his life more deeply.

  As I reflect on Peter's obedience, I realize that I must give priority to God's Word and obey it above my own experience and understanding. When I focus too much on my own efforts and knowledge, I can become proud and unwilling to receive God's instruction. I have come to recognize this attitude in my own life.

  I have learned that I need to acknowledge there are things I do not yet know and learn to trust and obey God's Word.
