reflection_en: >-
  Have you ever spent much of your life pursuing a particular goal or purpose?
  In my own life, although I had the assurance of salvation through God's grace,
  I did not clearly understand the purpose of my life. I used to think that
  having great ambitions and striving to become a successful person in the
  future was what life was really about. At the same time, I often found myself
  worrying, asking, “Will I really succeed?” and “Will my dreams and ambitions
  ever come true?”



  The truth is, I did not understand God's purpose for my life. As a result, I mistakenly assumed that the best life I could imagine for myself must also be God's will. But as I reflected on Romans 8:29, I began to understand that God had not only planned to save me but had also predestined me to become more like His Son.



  From that point on, I gradually began to understand that the meaning of life is not about creating the life we want for ourselves, but about surrendering ourselves to God's purpose and living accordingly. This understanding gave me courage. It did not mean that all my fears disappeared, but fear no longer had the right to rule my life. As I continued to walk under the guidance and light of God's Word, the path I needed to follow became clearer, and I began to experience greater security and confidence in my life.
